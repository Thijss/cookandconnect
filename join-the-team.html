Join the Cook & Connect team

Cook & Connect is a community initiative in Amsterdam that brings locals and refugees together through shared dinners at people’s homes. Many refugees living in COA centers are not allowed to cook for themselves — something they deeply miss. Through cooking and eating together, we create moments of connection, cultural exchange, understanding, and belonging.

What started as small dinner evenings is slowly growing into a wider movement focused on bringing back humanity in a society that often feels increasingly divided and disconnected. To help Cook & Connect grow sustainably and reach more people, we are currently looking for volunteers who would like to contribute their skills, ideas, energy, and time to the project.

We are currently looking for people who can help with:

Marketing & Social Media

Help us grow the visibility of Cook & Connect and spread the story behind the project.

This could include:

  • Creating content for Instagram and LinkedIn
  • Helping with branding and storytelling
  • Social media strategy
  • Growing online visibility
  • Photography/video content
  • SEO knowledge is a huge plus!

Communication & Community Building

Help us build partnerships, strengthen the community, and connect with organizations and participants.

This could include:

  • Outreach and communication
  • Writing emails and newsletters
  • Building partnerships with local organizations
  • Helping manage community communication
  • Storytelling and media outreach

Subsidies, Funding & Grants

As Cook & Connect grows, sustainable funding will help us organize more dinners, reach more people, and create long-term impact.

We are looking for people with experience or interest in:

  • Grant writing
  • Subsidy applications
  • Fundraising
  • Social impact funding
  • Researching opportunities for financial support

Every Form of Help is Welcome

You do not need to commit huge amounts of time to make a difference. Even helping out occasionally for a few hours, sharing ideas, using your expertise once in a while, or supporting the project behind the scenes can already be incredibly valuable.

Cook & Connect is growing organically, and every contribution — big or small — genuinely helps.

What you get in return

While these are volunteer positions, we hope the experience itself feels meaningful and rewarding.

By joining Cook & Connect, you become part of a warm, creative, and purpose-driven community where human connection is central.

You will have the opportunity to:

  • Make a real social impact
  • Meet people from different cultures and backgrounds
  • Contribute to a more connected and compassionate society
  • Be part of a growing grassroots initiative
  • Help shape the future of the project together

Whether you can help occasionally or would like to be more actively involved — we would love to hear from you.

Let’s bring back humanity, learn from each other and exchange cultures.
