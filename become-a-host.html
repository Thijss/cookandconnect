Become a host

Join our community and become a host to help to gap the bridge between two worlds.

Open up your kitchen for a night, and meet people who would never meet otherwise. Cook together, exchange cultures and build connections.

How it works

  1. Sign up
    Fill in the registration form.
  2. We get in touch
    We’ll contact you to get to know you and answer any questions you might have.
  3. Share your availability
    Let us know how many people you can host and when you’re available.
  4. Get matched
    We match you with a small group that fits your availability.
  5. Choose the dish
    The group will choose a dish from their home country and create a shopping list.
  6. Get connected
    A few days before the dinner, we create a WhatsApp group with you and the participants. This is where everyone gets introduced and the shopping list is shared.
  7. Open your home
    On the agreed date, you welcome the group into your home. Together, you cook, share a meal, and spend the evening connecting.

What it means

Hosting is a simple, yet powerful way to make a difference.

For many participants, having a place to call home — even for just one evening — is something they deeply miss. Being welcomed into a real home, cooking their own food, and sharing it with others can mean a lot to them.

It’s a warm, human experience — a small gesture, with a big impact.
